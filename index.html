Healthcare | Heuristic Evaluation

CloudNine Hospital App — Heuristics Evaluation

Self-Initiated Project | Healthcare UX

Problem

CloudNine Hospital's app offers online services (consultation booking, lab tests, scans, medicine orders) but users faced friction during the most critical task — booking an in-person consultation. The app needed a systematic usability audit to identify design problems against established heuristics.

My Role

Solo researcher conducting a full heuristic evaluation. Evaluated the "Booking in-person consultation" task flow against Jakob Nielsen's 10 usability heuristics, assigning severity ratings (0–4) to each finding.

Methods Used

Heuristic Evaluation Jakob Nielsen's 10 Heuristics Severity Rating (0–4) Task Flow Analysis

Key Findings (Severity Breakdown)

0: No problem (2) 1: Cosmetic (1) 2: Minor (2) 3: Major (3) 4: Catastrophe (1)
  • Visibility of System Status (Severity 3): "Select Consultation Type" button didn't show types upfront — users had to click to discover options. No progress bar to indicate remaining steps.
  • Match with Real World (Severity 3): "PayNow" button actually opened payment options (not immediate payment), causing confusion and potential drop-off.
  • Help & Documentation (Severity 4): Support page took multiple clicks to reach and contained inaccurate booking process info — evidenced by 5 user dislikes on the process.
  • Consistency (Severity 2): Mismatched icons for same services across Home and Help & Support pages.
  • Aesthetic Design (Severity 2): Non-intuitive city icons and overly verbose doctor profile spread across 3 pages.

Impact

9 usability issues identified | 5 actionable fixes prioritized

Reflection

Even well-established healthcare apps have fundamental usability gaps. The "PayNow" mislabel was a classic example of how a single button can create false expectations and drive users away. Severity ratings helped stakeholders prioritize fixes by business risk, not just design preference.

Healthcare

Digital Health Platform UX Research

US Pharmacopeia | 2022 – 2023

Problem

Healthcare professionals struggled to access and navigate USP's digital reference standards platform, leading to low adoption and frustrated users seeking competitor solutions.

My Role

Led end-to-end UX research as Senior Manager, Global Market Insights. Managed a cross-functional team of researchers and collaborated with product and design teams.

Methods Used

User Interviews Journey Mapping Usability Testing Competitive Analysis Surveys

Key Findings

  • Users spent 40% more time searching for standards than on competitor platforms
  • Information architecture didn't match mental models of lab technicians vs. quality managers
  • Mobile access was critical for field-based quality inspectors

Impact

Redesigned IA adopted | 35% task completion improvement

Reflection

Segmenting users by role (not just title) was critical. What a lab tech needs at 9 AM differs drastically from what a quality manager needs at 5 PM.

AdTech

Campaign Effectiveness & Brand Lift Research

InMobi | 2025 – Present

Problem

Advertisers lacked clear understanding of how InMobi's mobile ad campaigns influenced brand perception and user behavior beyond click-through rates.

My Role

Manager, Consumer Insights. Designed and executed brand lift and attitude-behaviour studies. Leveraged GenAI to automate reporting and enhance survey frameworks.

Methods Used

Brand Lift Studies Attitude-Behaviour Surveys A/B Testing Generative AI Data Analytics

Key Findings

  • Video ad formats drove 3x higher brand recall than static banners
  • Attitude shifts didn't correlate with immediate purchase intent — a 2-week lag existed

Impact

Healthcare / Life Sciences

Pharma Product Uptake & Gap Analysis

Optimal Strategix Group | 2016 – 2018

Problem

A pharmaceutical client needed to understand why their new drug wasn't achieving projected market uptake despite strong clinical trial results.

My Role

Senior Associate leading full-cycle CX research. Managed qualitative and quantitative studies across multiple markets to inform design and customer strategy.

Methods Used

In-Depth Interviews Max-Diff Analysis Ethnography Competitive Analysis Journey Mapping

Key Findings

  • Physicians weren't rejecting the drug — they simply didn't know when to prescribe it over existing options
  • Patient education materials were too clinical, failing to address real-world adherence concerns
  • Pharmacists were the hidden influencers — 70% of patients asked them for recommendations

Impact

Repositioning strategy adopted | 28% uptake increase in 6 months

Reflection

Never underestimate the "middleman" in healthcare. The patient journey has more stakeholders than you initially map — always dig deeper.
